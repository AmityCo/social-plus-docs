The **Content** page shows how much content your community produces, how far it travels, and which formats and individual posts earn the most engagement.

<img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/dashboard-new/content-overview.png" alt="Content page showing KPI cards for posts, comments, reactions and mentions, posts and engagement chart, reach and impressions chart, and engagement by content type" />

<Note>
Widget data is limited to the communities assigned to your account. Use **View community access** in the page banner to see which communities you can analyze.
</Note>

## Key metrics

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Total posts** | Posts published during the period. |
| **Comments / Replies** | Comments and replies created during the period. |
| **Reactions** | Reactions given during the period. |
| **Mentions** | Times users mentioned others in content. |

Each card shows the change versus the previous period of equal length.

## Filtering

Use **Filter** to scope the whole page by:

- **Community** — limit metrics to one or more communities.
- **User tag** — count only content from users with matching tags.
- **Role** — count only content from users with matching roles.

## Content overview tab

### Posts & engagement

A daily trend chart with toggleable series for **Posts**, **Comments**, **Reactions**, and **Mentions**. Use it to see whether engagement (comments, reactions) keeps pace with content volume — engagement growing faster than post count is a sign of a healthy, conversational community.

### Reach & impressions

Daily **Reach** (unique users who saw content) and **Impressions** (total views, including repeats). A widening gap between impressions and reach means the same users are viewing content repeatedly; flat reach with rising post volume means new content isn't finding new audiences.

### Engagement by content type

Compares performance across every post format — **Text**, **Image**, **Video**, **Poll**, **File**, **Live stream**, **Clip**, **Audio**, and **Custom** — showing post volume and the engagement each format generates. Switch between **Number** and **Percentage** views. Formats with no posts in the selected range are flagged, so gaps in your content mix are easy to spot.

<Tip>
If a format earns outsized engagement relative to its volume (for example, few video posts but high engagement per post), that's a cue to encourage more of it — through prompts, creator programs, or featured placement.
</Tip>

## Post summary tab

A searchable, sortable table of individual posts published in the period.

<img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/dashboard-new/content-post-summary.png" alt="Post summary tab listing posts with author, community, post type, created date, last activity, reach, impressions, reactions, comments, flags, and engagement rate" />

Narrow the table with the **content type** dropdown (for example, only polls or only live streams) or search for a specific post. Each row shows the post preview (author, community, format, and excerpt) alongside:

- **Created date** and **Last activity** — how long a post keeps generating engagement.
- **Reach** and **Impressions** — distribution performance.
- **Reactions**, **Comments**, and **Flags** — engagement and moderation signals.
- **Engagement rate** — engagement relative to reach, the best single measure for comparing posts of different sizes.

<Tip>
Sort by **Flags** to triage content that may need moderator review, or by **Engagement rate** to find repeatable content patterns worth promoting.
</Tip>

## Comparing posts

Use the checkbox on each row to select posts, then select **Compare** in the bar that appears at the bottom of the table.

<img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/dashboard-new/content-post-compare.png" alt="Post summary table with two posts selected and a floating bar showing the selection count and a Compare button" />

The comparison view places the selected posts side by side — author, publish date, title, and format — and charts their performance against each other across four metrics:

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Reactions** | Number of reactions on these posts. |
| **Comments** | Number of comments on these posts. |
| **Reach** | Unique members who saw these posts. |
| **Impressions** | Total views across these posts. |

The comparison has its own date-range picker, so you can widen or narrow the window independently of the main page — useful for comparing posts published weeks apart over their full lifetime. Use **Clear all** to reset the selection and start a new comparison.

<Tip>
Compare a high-performing post against an average one of the same format to isolate what made it work — timing, community, or framing. Comparing across formats (for example, a poll versus a live stream announcement) shows which format earns more engagement for the same topic.
</Tip>

## Exporting

The page-level **Export** menu lets you pick any combination of widgets — Posts & engagement, Reach & impressions, Engagement by content type — and download them as CSV in one go. Individual widgets also offer their own **Export CSV** button.
