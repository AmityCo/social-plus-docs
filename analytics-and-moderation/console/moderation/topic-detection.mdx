---
title: "Topic detection"
description: "Describe the topics your community shouldn't discuss, in your own words, and let AI find them in posts, comments, messages, and videos"
---

Some content breaks your community guidelines without being hateful or explicit: spoilers, competitor promotion, unverified health claims, or sensitive local issues. Topic detection lets you describe these topics in your own words. AI then looks for them in posts, comments, messages, and videos, and your [Rule builder](/analytics-and-moderation/console/moderation/rule-builder) rules decide what happens to a match.

<Info>
  Topic detection is turned on per network and is part of the AI Moderation add-on. If the page shows **Access required**, contact social.plus support. Finding topics in videos is turned on separately.
</Info>

<Note>
  These moderation topics are different from the topics on the [Sentiment analysis](/analytics-and-moderation/dashboard-new/sentiment-analysis) dashboard. Dashboard topics are found automatically to show what people talk about and don't moderate anything.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a topic">
    On the **Topic detection** page, name the topic and describe what it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a rule">
    In **Rule builder**, add a topic condition, pick the topic, choose how likely a match must be, and choose the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matches">
    Content the rule acts on appears in the [moderation feed](/analytics-and-moderation/console/user-and-content-management/mod-feed) as **Flagged by moderation rule**, with **Text matched a topic detection** or **Video matched a topic detection** and the topic name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A topic on its own does nothing. You decide how strict to be and what to do in each rule, so one topic can flag content in one rule and delete it in another.

## Before you start

Open **Moderation → Topic detection** in the console sidebar.

| Your role includes | You can |
| --- | --- |
| **Can view moderation feed** | See the topic list. |
| **Can manage moderation settings** | Also create, edit, and delete topics. |

## Create a topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Create topic**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the topic">
    Enter a short, clear name, for example `Movie spoilers`. You'll pick topics by this name in Rule builder. Names must be unique.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    In **Description**, explain what the topic covers. The AI reads this description to decide whether content matches, so it matters more than the name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create topic**. The topic is ready to use in Rule builder straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Write a good description

Write the description as if you were briefing a new moderator who doesn't know your community:

- Say what the topic includes, with examples.
- Say what it doesn't include when the line matters.
- Use the words your members use.

| Topic | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Movie spoilers | Posts or messages that reveal plot twists, endings, or character deaths from films or series released in the last three months. Reviews that warn readers about spoilers first are fine. |
| Competitor promotion | Content that recommends or links to other fitness apps or coaching services, or asks members to move to them. Mentioning a competitor in a general discussion is fine. |

<Tip>
  Try a new topic in a test-mode rule first. If the rule's **Activity** tab shows matches you didn't expect, tighten the description.
</Tip>

## Edit or delete a topic

- **Edit**: select the edit icon on the topic's row. You can change the description but not the name. To rename a topic, create a new one, switch your rules to it, and then delete the old one.
- **Delete**: select the delete icon and confirm. You can't delete a topic that any rule uses, even an inactive or test-mode rule. Remove the topic from those rules, or delete the rules, first.

## Use topics in rules

Rule builder has two topic conditions:

| Condition | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| **Contain text that matches a topic detection** | The text of posts, comments, and messages |
| **Contains a video that matches a topic detection** | Videos in posts. Not available for messages. |

In the condition, pick one or more topics and a **Likelihood**: **Very unlikely**, **Unlikely**, **Possible**, **Likely**, or **Very likely**. The rule matches content that's at least that likely to be about any of the topics.

- Choose **Likely** or **Very likely** for actions you can't undo, such as **Delete**, so only clear cases are removed.
- Choose **Possible** for **Flag for review**, so your team sees borderline content without it being removed.

Topic checks run just after content is published, so topic rules can flag, hide, or delete but not block:

| Target | Actions you can use |
| --- | --- |
| Posts and comments | Flag for review, Flag then hide post from feed, Delete |
| Messages | Flag for review, Delete |

Text is usually checked within seconds. Videos take longer.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Topics per network | 50. **Create topic** is unavailable at the limit until you delete one. |
| Topic name | 150 characters |
| Topic description | 1000 characters |

The topic list can be searched by name and shows 10, 20, or 50 topics per page.
