<Info>
  GIFs let your members react and post the way they already do in other apps. When your app sends GIFs from its own GIF provider, they arrive as GIF posts and GIF comments. Your moderators can see, filter, and remove them in the Console like any other content. This page covers the Console workflows, not developer implementation.
</Info>

## How a GIF Is Stored

A GIF is a **link**, not an uploaded file. social.plus stores the GIF's address exactly as it was sent and never downloads or copies it. Each GIF loads straight from the provider that hosts it, such as GIPHY.

A GIF link must:

- Use `https`
- End in `.gif` (any letter case)
- Be at most 2,000 characters
- Come from a host your network's [allowlist](/analytics-and-moderation/console/moderation/roles-and-privileges) doesn't block. A link from a blocked host is refused.

| Content | What it can hold |
| --- | --- |
| **GIF comment or reply** | One GIF, with or without text. A GIF can't sit beside a photo or video in the same comment. |
| **GIF post** | 1–10 attachments in total. Each GIF can appear only once. GIFs can sit beside photos, videos, files, clips, or audio. |

GIFs carry no alt text and no product tags.

<Note>
  Members create GIF comments and GIF posts from your app. In the Console, admins can create GIF posts ([see below](#creating-a-gif-post)) but can't add a GIF to a comment or reply.
</Note>

## Viewing GIFs

GIFs appear in the post list, the comment panel, the **Comments & Replies** tab, the [Moderation Feed](/analytics-and-moderation/console/user-and-content-management/mod-feed), and a user's history.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="GIF tile">
    - Each GIF shows as a tile with a **GIF** badge.
    - Lists show the GIF's first frame. The GIF plays when you hover over or focus the tile.
    - A loading placeholder shows until the GIF arrives from its provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Full-size preview">
    Click **Preview** on a tile to open the GIF at full size. The preview plays the GIF and shows:
    - **Source**: the link's host and path, plus the GIF's size in pixels (for example `480 × 270`) when known
    - **Open link**: opens the stored GIF link in a new tab
    - **Delete this GIF**: shown on a post's GIF only, and only to admins who can moderate posts and comments ([see below](#deleting-a-gif))
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Unavailable">
    If the GIF's link no longer loads, for example because the provider removed the GIF, the tile shows **Unavailable**. The Console doesn't refresh or repair the link. You can still open the preview, open the link, and delete the GIF.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deleted GIF comment">
    When a GIF comment is deleted, its tile shows as **Deleted**. The GIF itself is no longer shown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Moderating GIFs

### Finding GIFs in the Moderation Feed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Moderation Feed">
    Go to Sidebar → Moderation → Moderation Feed, then open **To Review** → **Posts & Comments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by Content Type">
    Open the advanced filter. Under **Attachments**, select **GIFs** to find GIF posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find GIF Comments">
    GIF comments and replies are listed under **Comments & replies**, not under **GIFs**. The **GIFs** option finds posts only, just as **Photos** and **Videos** do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **AI moderation doesn't scan GIFs.** An AI **Passed** or **Flagged** result on a post or comment that holds a GIF covers only its text. To catch harmful GIFs, rely on user reports and your GIF provider's content filter.
</Warning>

On these items, the **AI Moderation** row has an info icon. Hover over the row, or focus the icon, to see which part AI moderation checked:

| AI result | Tooltip |
| --- | --- |
| Passed | *AI moderation checked the text. GIFs aren't scanned.* |
| Flagged | *AI moderation flagged the text. GIFs aren't scanned.* |

The icon shows in the Moderation Feed, on post and comment cards, on the Comments & Replies tab, and in a post's comment panel. It doesn't show while the AI result is still pending.

### Deleting a GIF

| What you delete | How | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One GIF in a post** | Open the GIF's preview → **Delete this GIF**, or use the GIF's own delete action on the post card, then confirm | Only that GIF is removed. The post and its other attachments stay. |
| **A whole GIF post** | Delete the post as usual ([Moderation Actions](/analytics-and-moderation/console/social-management/social-management/posts#moderation-actions)) | The post and all its GIFs are removed. |
| **A GIF comment or reply** | Delete the comment or reply as usual ([Moderation Actions](/analytics-and-moderation/console/social-management/social-management/comments#moderation-actions)) | The comment and its GIF are removed. A comment's GIF can't be deleted on its own. |

In the Moderation Feed, **Delete** and **Clear flag** work on GIF posts and comments the same way they work on other content.

<Info>
  Reading GIF content needs the same permission as the Moderation Feed or Posts & Comments. Deleting a GIF needs permission to moderate posts and comments. See [Roles & Privileges](/analytics-and-moderation/console/moderation/roles-and-privileges).
</Info>

## Creating a GIF Post

The Console has no built-in GIF search. Copy the GIF's link from your GIF provider, then paste it into the post composer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Composer">
    In Posts and comments, click **Create post** → **Post**, then choose the destination and identity as usual ([Creating Posts](/analytics-and-moderation/console/social-management/social-management/posts#creating-posts)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the GIF Link Panel">
    Click **Add GIF by link**, next to the other media buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Link">
    Paste the GIF link into **GIF link**. The Console checks the link as you type and loads a preview, with the host and size below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the GIF">
    Once the preview loads, click **Add** or press Enter. The GIF joins the post's media list, and the field clears so you can add another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Finish the post, then **Post** or **Schedule** it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The GIF link panel with a link pasted and its preview loaded. The host and size show under the preview.">
  <img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/console/create-post-gif-link-preview.png" alt="Add GIF by link panel showing a pasted GIF link, the Add button, and a loaded preview" />
</Frame>

**Add** stays off until the link is valid and its preview has loaded. The panel shows why:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| *Use an https link that ends in .gif.* | The link doesn't meet the [link rules](#how-a-gif-is-stored). |
| *Couldn't load this GIF. Check the link and try again.* | The link is valid, but the GIF didn't load from its host. |
| *This GIF is already in the post.* | That link is already attached. Each GIF can appear in a post only once. |

<Frame caption="A link that isn't https or doesn't end in .gif turns the field red, and Add stays off.">
  <img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/console/create-post-gif-link-invalid.png" alt="GIF link panel showing the error Use an https link that ends in .gif" />
</Frame>

<Frame caption="After you click Add, the GIF joins the media list with its file name, host, and size. Click × to remove it.">
  <img src="/images/analytics-and-moderation/console/create-post-gif-added.png" alt="Media list showing an added GIF row with its name, host, and size" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Mixing GIFs with other media">
    A GIF can sit beside photos, videos, files, or audio in the same post, and adding a GIF doesn't turn off the other media buttons. The 10-attachment limit counts every attachment, GIFs included. At 10, **Add GIF by link** turns off and the composer shows *You have reached the limit of 10 media for one post.*

    <Warning>
      A post that mixes media types, such as a GIF beside a photo, shows only in apps on newer SDK versions. Older SDKs and UIKit versions leave mixed posts out of feeds. For the widest reach, keep a post to one media type.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No product tags or alt text">
    You can't tag products on a GIF or give a GIF alt text. Those options appear on photos and videos only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Editing a GIF post">
    When you edit a post, its GIFs load back into the media list. Remove a GIF row to delete that GIF when you save. GIFs you keep are unchanged, including the size the app sent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely cause | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A GIF tile shows **Unavailable** | The provider removed the GIF or changed its link | Open the link to check it. Delete the GIF if it's no longer needed. |
| **Add** stays off after you paste a link | The link isn't `https`, doesn't end in `.gif`, or didn't load | Copy the direct `.gif` link from your provider, not the provider's page link. |
| The post is refused when you publish | The GIF's host is blocked by your allowlist | Add the host to your [allowlist](/analytics-and-moderation/console/moderation/roles-and-privileges) or use a GIF from an allowed host. |
| **GIFs** filter shows no GIF comments | The **GIFs** filter finds GIF posts only | Use **Comments & replies** to review GIF comments. |
