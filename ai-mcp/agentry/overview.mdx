Agentry is how brands use social.plus in the AI-native era. Every post, comment, and reaction your app generates is first-party data — a signal about what your users care about and how they behave. Agentry puts that data to work where your team already is: ask in plain English from ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI app, and get back a clear summary plus the underlying data, ready to tabulate, chart, or drill into with follow-up questions.

## What you can ask

Agentry reasons over your network's engagement activity — posts, comments, reactions, stories, events, users, and communities — as counts, trends, comparisons, cohorts, and rankings across roughly the last 90 days. If a question combines those ingredients — *what happened, measured how, over which period, compared to what* — Agentry can usually answer it.

Simple questions work:

- *How many users were active yesterday?*

And so do the composite ones each team actually brings:

- **Marketing** — *I'm prepping a campaign brief: which content formats drove the most engagement last month, and which communities engaged the most?*
- **Product** — *We launched a feature last Tuesday. Compare daily active users and posting volume in the week after versus the week before.*
- **Community managers** — *Which communities have gone quiet this month, and how does their new-member activation compare to the network overall?*

Every answer comes back as a one-line summary plus structured data, so your AI app can build tables and charts from real numbers — and you can keep asking.

Out of scope, by design: activity older than the data window, anything outside your own network, and acting on your app — Agentry answers questions; it doesn't post, moderate, or administer.

## How it works

- **Read-only by design.** Agentry answers questions by reading your analytics data. It never posts, modifies, or deletes anything in your app.
- **Your data stays yours.** Each connection is scoped to your own network's data and authorized by your administrator from the Console. Admins see every connection and can remove any of them at any time.
- **Fresh, bounded data.** Activity data covers roughly the last 90 days and refreshes daily.
- **Self-contained questions.** Each question is answered on its own, so include the context that matters — time ranges, baselines, comparisons. AI apps handle this naturally when you ask follow-ups in the same conversation.
