Every post, comment, and reaction in your community is a signal about what your members care about. Agentry turns that first-party data into answers: ask in plain English from the AI app your team already uses, and get back a clear summary plus the underlying data — ready to tabulate, chart, or drill into with follow-up questions.

<Info>
  Agentry is available per network. If the Agentry page in your Console says it isn't enabled, contact support to turn it on.
</Info>

## What you can ask

```text
Which communities grew fastest this month?
```

```text
What share of new members posted within their first week?
```

```text
Compare reaction rates on posts vs. stories over the last 30 days
```

```text
We launched a new feature last Tuesday. How did activity change in the days after, compared to the week before?
```

Every answer comes back as a one-line summary plus structured rows, so the AI app can build tables and charts from real numbers — and you can keep asking.

## How it works

- **One read-only tool.** Agentry exposes a single tool, `ask_insights`, that answers each question against your community's analytics data. It reads; it never posts, modifies, or deletes anything.
- **Your data stays yours.** Each connection is scoped to exactly one community and authorized by your administrator from the social.plus Console. Admins see every connection and can remove any of them at any time.
- **Fresh, bounded data.** Activity data covers roughly the last 90 days and refreshes daily.
- **Self-contained questions.** The tool keeps no memory between questions, so include the context that matters — time ranges, baselines, comparisons — in each ask. AI apps handle this naturally when you ask follow-ups in the same conversation.

## Who it's for

- **Community managers** — engagement health, growth, and what's resonating, without a dashboard request.
- **Marketing** — content-format performance and campaign-window comparisons for briefs and reports.
- **Product teams** — before/after reads on launches, activity trends, and member behavior.
