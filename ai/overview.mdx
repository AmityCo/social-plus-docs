social.plus is the engagement infrastructure behind consumer apps — and every interaction it powers generates first-party data and insight your business owns. AI turns both into leverage. Teams ask their own data questions in the AI apps they already use, and AI coding agents build on the platform with the same grounding a senior integrator would have.

## Which one do you need?

| You want to… | Use |
|---|---|

They compose: build with Vise, keep the docs server connected in every editor, and give marketing and community teams Agentry for the questions that used to become dashboard requests.

## Engagement, insight, intelligence

- **Your data answers back.** Agentry turns the first-party data your app already generates — posts, comments, reactions, users, communities — into a conversation. Read-only, scoped to your own network, authorized by your admins.
- **The docs meet your tools where they are.** The documentation MCP server is a public, no-auth endpoint any MCP-capable tool can read, so assistants cite canonical guidance instead of guessing.
- **Building is verifiable.** Vise gives coding agents the project knowledge and verification steps to implement features that pass review, not just compile.

Owned engagement compounds: the experiences you power create the data, the data powers the answers, and the answers sharpen what you build next.
